# SLIDE 2 — PROBLEM + SOLUTION

## SkillTrack 360°: From Training Outcomes to Actionable Intelligence

## LEFT — REAL-WORLD ISSUE

### Real-world issue
Skilling systems can capture **enrolment, attendance, assessment and certification**, but reliable information about what happens **after training** can remain incomplete.

This makes it difficult to know:
- Who actually got employed?
- Who became self-employed or joined an apprenticeship?
- Who retained the job?
- Did wages improve?
- Were acquired skills actually relevant?
- **Why did some trainees remain unplaced?**

### WHY IMPORTANT?
# Training Completion ≠ Livelihood Success

Without longitudinal outcome tracking:
- Skill gaps remain hidden
- Non-placement reasons remain unclear
- Training relevance is difficult to measure
- Provider accountability becomes difficult
- Government interventions may not reach the right problem

## CENTER — OUR SOLUTION

# SLIDE 3 — TECHNICAL APPROACH

# TECHNICAL APPROACH

## METHODOLOGY & PROCESS OF IMPLEMENTATION

### 1. CONSENT & ENROLMENT
Create a verified trainee record.

### 2. OUTCOME PASSPORT
Create a longitudinal career/outcome profile.

### 3. SMART FOLLOW-UP
Collect employment, self-employment, apprenticeship and retention signals.

### 4. VERIFICATION & DATA QUALITY
Employer verification + source status + duplicate/conflict detection.

### 5. ANALYTICS
Identify:
- Outcome gaps
- Non-placement reasons
- Attrition
- Skill gaps
- District/course/provider trends

### 6. PREDICTION
Identify trainees/cohorts at higher **placement or retention risk**.

### 7. INTERVENTION
Recommend targeted actions.

### 8. IMPACT MEASUREMENT
Measure whether the intervention improved outcomes.
