## LEFT — REAL-WORLD ISSUE

### Training Data Exists. Outcome Data Doesn't Always.

Skilling programs can capture:

**Enrolment • Attendance • Assessment • Certification**

But reliable post-training information can remain incomplete:

- Employment
- Self-employment / Apprenticeship
- Job retention
- Wage progression
- Skill relevance
- Reasons for non-placement

### WHY IMPORTANT?

# TRAINING COMPLETION ≠ LIVELIHOOD SUCCESS

Without longitudinal outcome tracking:

- Skill gaps remain hidden
- Non-placement reasons remain unclear
- Training relevance is difficult to measure
- Providers cannot take targeted corrective action
- Government decisions become less evidence-driven
